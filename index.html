Step one is Build. And building starts with a problem, not with code. A real problem is one a real person has today, and you can tell when it's fixed.
Don't wait for someone to hand you one. They're all around you.
[CLICK] Where do you find them? Apps you use every day: where did you get stuck? That annoyance is useful. Open source projects: the issues list is people writing down what's broken. Tutorials: follow the steps, and where they break, that's a problem. Hackathons: read how you'll be judged before you build anything. Bounties: paid tasks posted online, with a clear finish line.
[CLICK] Before you build, ask who this affects. Talk to one person who has the problem, if you can.
[CLICK] Then make it small. "Fix sign-up for every app in Nigeria." Too big. You'll never finish, and nobody can tell if you did. "New users can't finish this app's sign-up on Android." You can find out why, fix it and test it. Someone can check it.
Here's the test. Write your problem in one sentence. If nobody can check it, make it smaller.
